The story

Why I built it.

I try to learn something new about football every day. Honestly, the hardest part is finding new content to learn from.

So I built ChalkTalkFB. It serves up a fresh concept every day and keeps changing the topic: a coverage on Sunday, a passing concept on Monday, a position on Tuesday, and so on. I wanted it to be more entertaining than reading something, so every idea gets drawn up on a chalkboard and explained in under a minute.

And yes, it's built with AI. I'm not hiding that. It's the whole point. AI helps write and fact-check every script, gives it a voice, animates the chalk, checks the result, and gets it scheduled. That's how one person can put out a new Short every single day.

ChalkTalkFB is just for fun, a way to bring together two things I love: AI and football. Every Short is something I'm learning, and sharing it is the best part.

How it works

From idea to upload, automated.

Six steps, every day, mostly run by AI. A daily automation builds the next Short each morning and keeps about a week in the queue.

  1. 01{{icon:bulb}}

    Topic

    An AI assistant picks the day's concept to fit the theme, with no repeats.

  2. 02{{icon:pen}}

    Script

    Written and fact-checked by AI. One concept, under 60 seconds.

  3. 03{{icon:mic}}

    Voice

    Kokoro open-source text-to-speech, with custom pronunciation fixes like “DEE-fense.”

  4. 04{{icon:board}}

    Chalk animation

    Python draws the field, X's, O's, and arrows in chalk. ffmpeg renders it.

  5. 05{{icon:shield}}

    QA

    Automated speech-to-text checks catch clipped or misheard words.

  6. 06{{icon:cal}}

    Schedule

    Hosted on Cloudflare Pages and queued to YouTube through Buffer for noon ET.
